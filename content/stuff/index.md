### Wonder Twin Powers Activate

{{<img-responsive src="/images/stuff/wonder_twin_powers_activate.jpg" class="img-left">}}

I was blogging before blogging was cool. Or even recognized. There were no social influencers and definitely no TikTok or Instagram. I think I still had my MySpace page. And started posting of Facebook when I was pregnant to show off my beautiful twin-filled belly. I used this blog as a creative outlet to document my experience of being a first time mom of twins. Plus, I just really like writing.

I’m glad I did this. My twins are now 16 and read the blog all of the time. It’s actually one of their favorite things to do.

http://jacobandmason.blogspot.com/2008/08/holy-shnikeys-we-have-two-babies.html
